# FOUNDING PRINCIPLES

---

## Why This Document Exists

This document records the foundational principles of the project.

Technology will evolve.

Programming languages will change.

AI models will change.

These principles should remain stable unless there is a compelling reason to change them.

---

# Principle 1

## We Build Trust

Our primary objective is not intelligence.

Our primary objective is trust.

Every architectural decision should strengthen trust.

---

# Principle 2

## AI Models Are Replaceable

Large Language Models are providers.

They are not the product.

The runtime must remain provider-agnostic.

---

# Principle 3

## The Runtime Is The Product

The value of the project is the runtime.

Not the model.

Not the prompt.

Not the provider.

The infrastructure is the product.

---

# Principle 4

## Every Important Action Is An Event

Events are the source of truth.

State is a projection.

History should never be lost.

---

# Principle 5

## Replay Before Assumptions

If something cannot be replayed,

it cannot be fully trusted.

Replay is a first-class capability.

---

# Principle 6

## Verification Before Release

Every release should verify:

- Replay
- Timeline
- Hash Chain
- Runtime Integrity

Verification is mandatory.

---

# Principle 7

## Explainability Is A Feature

The runtime should always answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- How did we get here?

Explainability is part of the product.

---

# Principle 8

## Small Stable Core

The Runtime Core should remain small.

New capabilities should be added as modules whenever possible.

Complexity belongs at the edges.

Stability belongs at the center.

---

# Principle 9

## Product Before Technology

We do not adopt technologies because they are popular.

We adopt technologies because they solve a real problem.

The mission is permanent.

Technology is temporary.

---

# Principle 10

## Long-Term Thinking

Shortcuts create technical debt.

Architecture creates longevity.

Every sprint should improve the future of the project.

---

# Principle 11

## Multi-Perspective Engineering

Major decisions benefit from multiple viewpoints.

Current engineering perspectives include:

- Product
- Architecture
- Code Review
- Research

Ideas are evaluated on merit.

The project is not driven by a single opinion.

---

# Principle 12

## Commercial Vision

The Event-Sourced AI Runtime is intended to become a commercial software platform.

Engineering quality is a business requirement.

Documentation is a business requirement.

Maintainability is a business requirement.

Trust is a business requirement.

---

# Product Identity

We are not building another chatbot.

We are not building another AI model.

We are building infrastructure that enables trustworthy AI.

---

# Mission Statement

Build infrastructure that allows people and organizations to trust artificial intelligence.
