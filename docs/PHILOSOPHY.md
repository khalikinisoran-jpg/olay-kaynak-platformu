# Project Philosophy

## Why?

We believe AI systems should be understandable, replayable and auditable.

An AI response should not disappear after it is generated.

Instead, every meaningful decision should become an immutable event.

---

## Our Principles

### Event First

Every important action is recorded as an event.

---

### Replay Everything

The system should be able to replay its history.

Understanding why an AI made a decision is a feature, not an afterthought.

---

### Provider Agnostic

The runtime should not depend on a single LLM provider.

OpenAI, DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini or local models should be interchangeable.

---

### Open Architecture

The core should be extensible.

New capabilities should be added as modules instead of modifying the core.

---

### Simplicity Wins

Simple systems survive.

Complexity must justify itself.

---

## Engineering Culture

We value:

- Evidence over opinions
- Experiments over assumptions
- Architecture over shortcuts
- Maintainability over cleverness
- Community over ego

---

## Long-Term Vision

Our goal is not to build another chatbot.

Our goal is to build an Event-Sourced AI Runtime capable of supporting reliable, auditable and collaborative AI systems.
